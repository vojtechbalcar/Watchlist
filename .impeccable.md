# Design context

## Users

People following stocks who want to understand whether a stock is beating its benchmark or simply moving with the market. The landing page introduces that distinction and leads to account creation.

## Brand personality

Clear, grounded, confident, and on the landing page also fun. Earlier feedback asked for a professional finance feel and expressive marketing composition, with less density than the application. In October 2026 the user called the open line-chart hero bland and asked for something more fun and visually appealing that still explains the idea.

## Aesthetic direction

The hero is a race. Oversized Helvetica sits on the white page, and below it a full-bleed dark track runs off both edges. Your stock and the S&P 500 leave the same start line, both run forward, and the market finishes ahead. The fun comes from motion and payoff: ticking readouts, speed lines, the finish markings, an underline that proves the headline, and a replay. It does not come from decoration. Maroon is for the brand and the primary action. Avoid panelled or bento heroes, literal application screenshots, busy isometric illustrations, and dashboard-like marketing panels.

## Design principles

- Make the benchmark distinction visible through one clear interaction.
- Use typography and composition to carry the hero; remove redundant decoration.
- Keep maroon for brand identity and green/red for market direction.
- Support touch, keyboard, visible focus, and reduced motion equally.
- Use illustrative data honestly, and preserve account-first CTA routing.
