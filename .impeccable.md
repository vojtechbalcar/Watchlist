# Design context

## Users

People following stocks who want to understand whether a stock is beating its benchmark or simply moving with the market. The landing page introduces that distinction and leads to account creation.

## Brand personality

Clear, grounded, confident. Prior user feedback calls for a professional finance feel and expressive marketing composition, with less density than the application.

## Aesthetic direction

The hero is open and has room to breathe: oversized Helvetica on the white page, no panels or boxes, and two lines for the stock and the S&P 500 drawn across generous space. The gap between them is the only accent. Maroon is for the brand and the primary action. Avoid panelled or bento heroes, literal application screenshots, busy isometric illustrations, and dashboard-like marketing panels.

## Design principles

- Make the benchmark distinction visible through one clear interaction.
- Use typography and composition to carry the hero; remove redundant decoration.
- Keep maroon for brand identity and green/red for market direction.
- Support touch, keyboard, visible focus, and reduced motion equally.
- Use illustrative data honestly, and preserve account-first CTA routing.
