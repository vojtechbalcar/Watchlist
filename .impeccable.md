# Design context

## Users

People following stocks who want to understand whether a stock is beating its benchmark or simply moving with the market. The landing page introduces that distinction and leads to account creation.

## Brand personality

Clear, grounded, confident, and on the landing page also calm and visually generous. Earlier feedback asked for a professional finance feel and expressive marketing composition, with less density than the application. In October 2026 the user rejected first the open line-chart hero (bland) and then the race hero. They asked for an illustration that breathes and is visually appealing, even if it explains the idea less literally.

## Aesthetic direction

The landing page follows Mobbin's `DESIGN.md`: gallery-white monochrome, ink on white, a neutral tint ladder instead of shadows, stadium-pill controls, 24px containers and 30% squircle tiles. The hero is an illustration: company tiles on hairline orbits around the S&P 500, drawn as a fan of twelve monthly petals stepping from light grey to ink. Sections below are minimal centred lockups, and the page ends in a compact soft footer card rather than a dark band. Maroon is the single brand accent (wordmark and headline periods); primary actions are ink pills. Avoid panelled or bento heroes, literal application screenshots, busy isometric illustrations, dashboard-like marketing panels, large return figures in marketing sections, and heavy dark bands.

## Design principles

- Let the illustration lead the hero; explain the benchmark distinction in plain words, not large figures.
- Use typography, space and composition to carry each section; remove redundant decoration.
- Keep maroon for brand identity and green/red for market direction.
- Support touch, keyboard, visible focus, and reduced motion equally.
- Use illustrative data honestly, and preserve account-first CTA routing.
